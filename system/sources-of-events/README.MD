# Sources of Events

This subdirectory provides a list of sources of events.  Every event has at least one source for the event. Can an event have more than one source?

[list-of-sources.md](list-of-sources.md)


This is a set of synthesized business events for a "lemonade stand".

#1: JE-201	2024-01-01	Investment by owner to open a lemonade stand. (Investment memorandum)

#2: JE-202	2024-01-02	Long term borrowing to purchase refrigerator. (Contract with bank)

#3: JE-203	2024-01-05	Purchase of refrigerator using cash from additional borrowings. (Sales receipt from vendor)

#4: JE-204	2024-01-10	Purchase of lemons to put into inventory on account. (Sales receipt from supplier)

#5: JE-205	2024-01-15	Payment for contractor to work the lemonade stand. (Contract)

#6: JE-206	2024-01-16	Sales on account. (Sales invoice)

#7: JE-206	2024-01-16	Costs related to sales on account. (Inventory adjustment memo; assume perpetual inventory)

#8: JE-207	2024-01-20	Collection of accounts receivable. (Check received from customer, deposited into bank.)

#9: JE-208	2024-01-21	Payment of accounts payable. (Invoice from vendor)

#10: JE-209	2024-01-25	Repayment of long term debt including accrued interest. (Loan contract with bank)

#11: JE-210	2024-01-24	Accrual of interest on debt. (Loan contract with bank)

#12: JE-223	2024-01-28	Closing entry. Inventory write offs, adjust to physical inventory. (Reconciliation to actual physical inventory)

#13: JE-224	2024-01-28	Closing entry. Depreciation and amortization expensed. (Depreciation schedule)

#14: JE-225	2024-01-28	Closing entry. Write of of PPE. (Inventory loss memorandum)

#15: JE-226	2024-01-29	Closing entry.  Book provision for income taxes. (Income tax provision schedule)
